## Preview image

Play a shift to the end and press **SAVE THIS NOTICE** on the Notice of Delay. It
downloads a 1200px PNG of that night's card — the game's own output, not concept art.

A grade D night makes a better cover than a grade A one: the card is fuller, the
delays are red, and the closing list reads "N THINGS NOBODY EVER SAID OUT LOUD".

## Description (492/500 characters)

```
Two strangers work a single-track railway from opposite ends. Neither can see the other's line, and each one holds the other's rulebook, so the only way through is to describe what you see.

The hard part was not the multiplayer. It was proving the game needs talking at all, so I built a planner to measure whether a pair who share nothing do worse. They do.

Every shift is generated and solved before it is printed, so the timetable is always achievable. Next: three and four signal boxes.
```
